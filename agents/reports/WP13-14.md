Each entry has `id`, `name`, `title`, `portraitKey`, `portraitPlaceholder`, `fact` (empty), `order`, `popup` and `educatorsName`.

  - No facts or quotes were written.
- **Readers.** Educators bios and names (`site.js`), the five mestre photo captions (`media.js`) and the `/about/abada` alt text (now `PHOTOS.mestraMarciaBerimbau.caption`) all read from the registry.
- **R13.3 `src/lib/game/mestres.js`.** Pure `mestreOfWeek(date)`: whole weeks since Monday 2026-09-28 on the New York calendar (via `nyDate` from `streaks.js`), mod 15, over `POPUP_MESTRES`. Dates before the epoch wrap to a valid index. A bare `'YYYY-MM-DD'` string is read as a New York date. It is not displayed anywhere.
- **Registry check in `scripts/content-check.mjs`.** Unique ids; name, title and educatorsName on every entry; numeric order; boolean popup; string fact; a real `PHOTOS` key or the placeholder flag; exactly 15 pop-up names with orders 1-15. I tested it by breaking the file: it reported the duplicate id, the missing title, "14 pop-up names" and the order gap. The Bira checks, the five-educators check and the summary line are unchanged.
- **Tests (`test/mestres.test.mjs`, 8 tests).**
  - The 15 exact names in order, unique ids, a portrait or placeholder each, empty facts.
  - The four portraits on hand, and Yara as Educators-only.
  - Educators and captions still read today's text.
  - 15 Mondays from 2026-W40 through 2026-W53 into 2027-W01 give all 15 names in order, and W02 wraps back to Bimba.
  - A window starting mid-rotation (W50) crosses New Year in order with each name once.
  - The week turns at Monday 00:00 New York time, both in EDT and after DST ends.
  - Every day of a week agrees, and dates before the epoch wrap.
